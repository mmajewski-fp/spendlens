## Summary

Implements the SpendLens wedge feature: a /recommendations page where a signed-in user with imported transactions and at least one active savings goal sees, per goal, a ranked list of expense-cutting suggestions tied to that goal's target and timeframe, plus excessive-spending alerts for categories above the income-relative threshold.
